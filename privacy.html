Privacy

Privacy information.

This page explains the website's current approach to personal information.

Information you provide

When you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, information you choose to send—such as your name, contact details and project or service enquiry—may be used to respond to you and manage the relevant business relationship.

Website operation

The hosting provider may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses, timestamps and browser details for security, reliability and service delivery. The site does not currently include an account system or public data-entry form.

Sharing

Personal information is not intended to be sold. It may be handled by service providers where necessary to operate the website or deliver requested services, subject to the applicable arrangements and law.

Retention and security

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected for, subject to legal or operational requirements. Reasonable safeguards should be used for information under the business's control.

Updates

This notice may be updated as the website gains contact forms, analytics or other features. Material changes should be reflected on this page.
