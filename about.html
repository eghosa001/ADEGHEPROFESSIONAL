Leadership

Founder & Principal

Omokaro Joy Adesuwa

Omokaro Joy Adesuwa is the Founder & Principal of Adeghe Professional Services. From Benin City, Edo State, she worked with LAPO Microfinance from 2007 to 2019, bringing more than a decade of experience in structured client service and operations to the business. She provides overall leadership for Adeghe's professional services and long-term direction.

Technology Lead

Fred Osawaru Eghosa

Fred Osawaru Eghosa leads Adeghe's software and digital development practice. From Benin City, Edo State, he is a 600-level dental student at the University of Benin and builds websites, mobile applications, business systems, automation and digital products with a focus on practical, maintainable solutions.
